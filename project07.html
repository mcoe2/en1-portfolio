Project 7 · October 2026

Draw a Square

For Project 7, I built and programmed a robot to draw a "perfect" square that had to land inside a 6-inch outer square and outside a 2.75-inch inner square. Each of us had one chance to draw it in class.

My in-class square checked off everything on the post-assessment rubric: it stayed on the placemat, stayed inside the solid outer square, and stayed outside the dashed inner square.
