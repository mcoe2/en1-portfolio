Project 9 · October 2026

Robotic Arm

For Project 9, everyone built a robotic arm to pass a ping-pong ball along. In class, all of our builds were lined up to see if the whole system could carry one ball from the first team to the last.

My build was a ramp with spinning LEGO pieces on it. The spinning pieces hit the ball and knock it along the ramp to pass it on.
