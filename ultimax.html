UltiMax

Play UltiMax free ultimax-frisbee.vercel.app →

UltiMax is a free pixel-art ultimate frisbee game I’m making in Godot 4 with GDScript. You control one player on a seven-person team in top-down matches against AI teams, and it runs right in the browser on computers, phones and tablets.

The game follows real ultimate rules: pulls, stall counts, turnovers, blocks, layouts and end zones. You can throw backhands, forehands, hammers and blades, choosing the curve and height by how you aim. The AI teams run vertical and horizontal stacks, handler resets and hucks on offense, and play person-to-person defense with a force.

Off the field, you manage a 12-player roster: edit names and roles, spend coins earned in matches to upgrade stats and sign players, and work your way up to the strongest teams, which unlock as you improve. An optional free account saves your team to the cloud.
