Project 4 · September 2026

Top Spinner

For Project 4, I designed and tested a spinning top that used gear ratios to control how fast the top spun. I explored how different gear combinations changed the speed and motion of the mechanism, and I adjusted the design to improve stability and performance. This project helped me connect mechanical design to the way forces and motion transfer through a system.
