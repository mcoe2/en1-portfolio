Biomimicry

For my biomimicry project, I designed a LEGO elephant trunk that mimics the way a real elephant uses its trunk to curl around and grip objects. The trunk is made from connected LEGO pieces that allow it to bend and curl around a branch. A motor controls the movement, demonstrating how a mechanical system can recreate the flexibility and gripping motion of an elephant’s trunk.
