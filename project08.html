Project 8 · October 2026

Astronaut Tools

For Project 8, I worked as a human factors design engineer to build a one-handed SPIKE Prime tool that an astronaut on a spacewalk could use to move a nut from one bolt to another. The tool had to work in either hand while wearing bulky ski gloves, stay in hand or tethered the whole time, and never let go of the nut, because a dropped nut floats away.

My tool used a wheel to spin the washer off the bolt.
